Features

Monitor Parking Spaces

This website helps parking lot owners track available and booked parking spaces in real-time.

  • IR Beam Breaker counts the number of cars entering the lot.
  • Ultrasonic sensors monitor specific parking spaces.
  • Future updates will track cars exiting as well.

Control Parking Traffic

Manage the LED lights at the entrance to indicate available spaces or during maintenance.

  • LEDs (red/green) indicate available parking spaces.
  • Switch LED lights remotely during maintenance periods.
  • Future plans include a driver app for booking spaces.
