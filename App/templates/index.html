Welcome to Park Smart

Revolutionizing parking management for lot owners with real-time monitoring and control.

  • This website will help parking lot owners monitor the number of parking spaces available and booked in the lot.
    • IR Beam Breaker is used to count the number of cars entering the parking lot.
    • Ultrasonic sensors monitor specific parking spaces inside the lot.
    • For future, two IR Beam Breakers could track cars exiting as well.
  • LEDs (red and green) at the entrance notify traffic about available parking spaces.
  • Control the LED light at the entrance via the website, e.g., switch to red during maintenance.
  • Future enhancements may include an app for drivers to view and book available parking spaces.
  • Target users: parking lot owners with two subscription levels:
    • Read Access: View car entries.
    • Read and Write Access: Control LEDs to manage traffic during maintenance.
